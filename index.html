Four Practical Lessons from Testing (Where Prompts Fail & How to Fix Them)

          Key takeaways for everyday AI use
        


        

          

            
1. Step-by-Step Prompts Stop Unit Mistakes

            

              With a basic Zero-Shot prompt, the AI forgot to convert Watts to Kilowatts in 2 out of 5 runs—producing an electricity bill 1,000× too high. Asking for step-by-step work (Chain-of-Thought) fixed the unit conversion and lifted accuracy from 62.2% to 78.4%.
            

          


          

            
2. Expert Personas Sound Confident Even When Wrong

            

              Telling the AI to act like a "Senior Editor" (Role Prompting) produced the best human writing scores (4.56 / 5), but made the AI sound authoritative even when its math was wrong (66.4% on Reasoning). Use personas for tone, not for math.
            

          


          

            
3. Avoid 5× Energy Waste on Simple Sorting

            

              Self-Consistency runs the AI 5 times per question, using 5.4× more tokens and electricity. While worthwhile on hard math (+6.2% accuracy), it only adds +0.3% on category sorting—where a 3-example prompt is 5× faster and cheaper.
            

          


          

            
4. Schema Templates Eliminate Code Crashes

            

              Under basic prompts, 7B–8B models wrap JSON data in polite sentences ("Here is your data..."), crashing automated code parsers 28.6% of the time. Enforcing a Structured-Output JSON template raises valid parsing to 98.2%.
